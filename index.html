Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026
Android application: cr.nivelfacil.easylevel

This policy explains how Easy Level (“the app”) handles information. “We” refers to the developer of Easy Level, who can be contacted at labsappcr@outlook.com.

At a glance

Level measurements are processed on your device. You do not need an account. Voice commands are optional. The app contains Google AdMob advertising, which involves data processing by Google and its advertising partners.

1. Measurements and device settings

Easy Level uses your device’s gravity sensor or accelerometer to calculate tilt, angles, and bubble position. These readings and your calibration reference are used locally to display measurements. The app does not send these measurements to our servers or keep a measurement history.

Your language and appearance preferences are saved on your device. Temporary interface state may be retained while the app is running or when the screen rotates. We do not operate a backend that stores your measurements or an Easy Level user profile.

2. Optional microphone and voice commands

If you enable voice commands, the app requests microphone permission so you can freeze a reading with a spoken command. This feature is optional; you can use the on-screen controls without microphone access.

The app uses Android’s speech recognition service. When supported, it selects on-device recognition. Otherwise, the speech service installed on your phone may transmit audio to its provider for processing, which may require an internet connection. The provider’s privacy policy and settings govern that processing.

Easy Level uses recognition results temporarily to detect commands. We do not create or store audio recordings or transcripts on our own servers. Listening ends when you turn it off, freeze the measurement, leave the app, or the recognition session ends or times out. You can revoke microphone permission in Android Settings → Apps → Easy Level → Permissions.

3. Advertising and consent

Easy Level displays banner ads using Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK collects and shares IP addresses, device or account identifiers, information about interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostic information. IP addresses may be used to estimate general location. These data support advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The app does not request precise GPS location.

Google and advertising partners process advertising data under their own policies. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage applicable consent and privacy choices. Where required and available, it presents a privacy message and provides an “Ad privacy” option to review your choices. Available options depend on your region and applicable requirements. Non-personalized ads may still require data for delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

4. Information you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot problems, and handle privacy requests. Our email service provider processes that correspondence. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

5. Data sharing and international processing

Data may be processed by Google and advertising partners for ads and consent, by your speech recognition provider when you use voice commands, and by our email provider when you contact us. These providers may process information in countries other than your own, under their respective privacy policies and applicable safeguards.

We may disclose correspondence or other information in our possession when legally required or when necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or protect legal rights.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Measurements and voice commands: the app does not maintain a persistent measurement, recording, or transcript history. Current readings and recognition results are used temporarily.
  • Local preferences: these remain until you change them, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Support emails: we keep correspondence for as long as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request its deletion by email.
  • Service provider data: advertising and speech service data are retained according to each provider’s policies and controls. Uninstalling Easy Level does not automatically delete information already held by these providers.

Easy Level does not offer user accounts, so there is no app account to delete.

7. Your choices and rights

You can disable voice commands, revoke microphone permission, clear local app data, and use the app’s “Ad privacy” control when available. Android also provides advertising privacy controls, such as resetting or deleting an advertising ID; availability and menu names vary by device.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal data, restrict or object to its processing, or withdraw consent. Contact us using the address below. We may need limited information to verify and respond to your request. For data controlled by an advertising or speech provider, you may also need to use that provider’s privacy tools. You may contact your local data protection authority with a complaint.

8. Security

Android’s application storage protections help protect local preferences. Google reports that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK are encrypted in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

9. Children’s privacy

Easy Level is a general-purpose measurement utility and is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its services change. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent.
